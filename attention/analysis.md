# Analysis

## Layer 2, Head 1

This head appears to connect determiners such as “the” and “a” with the nouns that they introduce. The pattern is not perfectly consistent, but it is noticeable when a determiner and its noun are separated from other likely targets by nearby words.

Example Sentences:
- The small dog carried a stick across the yard.
- A student placed the notebook beside the window.

## Layer 8, Head 4

This head appears to connect a pronoun with a noun phrase that gives it a likely referent. In the examples below, the pronouns “she” and “it” have plausible antecedents earlier in the sentence; the attention pattern can be noisy and does not by itself prove that the model has resolved the reference correctly.

Example Sentences:
- Maria dropped the glass because she was distracted.
- The book was on the desk, and it belonged to Sara.
